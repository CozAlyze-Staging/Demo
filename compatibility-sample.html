In Vedic astrology, compatibility starts with the Moon. The Moon shows the mind, the feelings and what each of you needs to feel at peace, so the tradition compares your two Moons first, through their signs and birth stars.

It then looks at both full charts: the seventh house of partnership and the planet that rules it, Venus and Jupiter, and the Navamsa, the chart Vedic astrologers read for marriage. Where temperaments differ, an argument may follow a pattern. One of you raises the issue sharply, and the other goes quiet to keep the peace. The quiet partner believes they are calming things down. This section looks at how the two of you might run that household together, through Bhakoot, the second house of savings and family resources, the eleventh house of gains, and Jupiter, the planet of growth and good fortune.

Many couples split along a familiar line. One partner feels secure with savings in place and a plan for the year. The other feels secure when life is lived well now, with room for generosity, travel and pleasure. In daily life this may show up as small tensions over a purchase, or over who notices the bills first.

A saver can keep a household steady, and a spender can keep it from becoming joyless. Problems come when one person starts carrying all the worry or all the blame.

What helps most is clarity. Decide together what security means for you, give each person some money of their own, and share the everyday work of the home fairly. In Vedic terms, a household runs well when each person honors their part of the duty.

Overview

Western astrology reads compatibility by laying one birth chart over the other. This is called synastry. It looks at how each person’s planets connect with the other’s: where they harmonize, where they create friction, and where one person’s planets land in the other’s life.

It also reads each chart on its own terms, because how you love starts with who you are. The Western view is psychological. It asks what each of you brings, what you stir up in each other, and how you can grow because of it.
