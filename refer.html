How It Works

  1. Share your link with someone new to CozAlyze.
  2. When they buy their first full reading, you earn $5.
  3. Every fifth customer you refer adds a $10 bonus.
  4. Once your balance reaches $25, it’s paid to you at the end of the month.

Program Rules

  • Only new CozAlyze customers count. Their first full reading qualifies: Your Vedic Reading, Your Tropical Reading, Your Combined Reading, or a package that includes one.
  • Each customer counts once. Compatibility readings, add-ons and later upgrades don’t earn another reward.
  • Refunded or charged-back purchases, referring yourself, and duplicate accounts don’t earn rewards.
  • Rewards are paid in cash once a month after the refund period, when your balance reaches $25. Smaller balances carry over to the next month.
  • Whenever you share your link, say that you earn a reward.
